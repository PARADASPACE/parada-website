About our team.

We are a team made up of students from POJFM High School interested in technology. Each of us is good at something else so we divided the work according to what we're good at so we can pack all the skills together.

The origin of our name comes from a Czech word expressing the positive attitude and great spirit of our team. We are ready to accepts challenges and overcome problems to reach our goals.
